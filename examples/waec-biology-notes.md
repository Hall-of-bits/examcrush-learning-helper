# Photosynthesis

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants convert light energy into chemical energy. Chlorophyll is the green pigment in chloroplasts that absorbs light. Carbon dioxide and water are the raw materials; glucose and oxygen are the products.

## Light-dependent reactions

The light-dependent reactions occur in the thylakoid membranes. ATP is the energy currency produced during the light stage. NADPH is a reduced coenzyme that carries electrons to the Calvin cycle.

## Calvin cycle

The Calvin cycle is the light-independent pathway that fixes carbon dioxide into sugars. Rubisco is the enzyme that catalyses the first step of carbon fixation. Stroma is the fluid region of the chloroplast where the Calvin cycle takes place.

# Respiration

Aerobic respiration is the breakdown of glucose in the presence of oxygen to release energy. Mitochondria are the organelles where most ATP from respiration is generated. Anaerobic respiration is respiration without oxygen and yields less ATP than aerobic respiration.
